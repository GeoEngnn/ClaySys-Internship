OLIPOP is a functional prebiotic soda brand founded in 2018 by Ben Goodwin and David Lester that reimagines classic soft drinks with gut-health benefits. Designed to deliver nostalgic flavors like Vintage Cola, Classic Root Beer, and Strawberry Vanilla without the heavy sugar load of traditional sodas, each can contains only 2 to 5 grams of sugar, 35 to 50 calories, and 6 to 9 grams of dietary fiber derived from a blend of prebiotics and botanicals such as chicory root, cassava fiber, marshmallow root, and slippery elm. By avoiding artificial sweeteners and focusing on digestive wellness, OLIPOP has become a leading name in the better-for-you beverage space, offering consumers a refreshing, low-sugar alternative that supports microbiome health.
